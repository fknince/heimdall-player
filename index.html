Heimdall Player

A free, ad-free player for LG webOS TVs. Add your own M3U playlist or Xtream-compatible account; live TV, TV guide, movies and series in one place.

Important: Heimdall Player does not provide or sell any channels or content. Use it only with sources you are entitled to watch.

Features

  • Live TV with a preview window, channel numbers, favourites
  • TV guide (EPG), movie and series pages, resume, next episode
  • Household profiles; a kids profile with a daily time limit and bedtime
  • Category locks with a parental PIN
  • Phone control: connect by QR on the same Wi-Fi, browse, play on the TV, use it as a remote
  • Turkish and English; webOS 3.0 and later
